# Action architecture improvement work

Status: **validated improvement**. On the reserved 400-seed confirmation panel, the combined policy improved sampled wins from 3.58% to 19.33%, a paired gain of 15.75 percentage points, 95% CI [13.54, 18.04]. Greedy wins improved from 7.75% to 32.12%, a gain of 24.38 points, CI [20.25, 28.50]. Both training replicates improved in both modes, and all pre-recorded acceptance requirements passed. The [complete numerical results](action_refactor_results.md) give counts, per-replicate intervals, ante-clear rates and provenance. PPO remains stopped. Earlier studies, datasets, checkpoints and logs remain preserved.

The candidate adds entity-aware Joker-sale scores and a learned correction over complete card subsets to the frozen epoch-7 checkpoint from `checkpoints/supervised_5k_v68_20260911_181359`. The matched control has the same original weights and a zero correction using the same sampler. Confirmation includes 4,800 physical games: 1,200 sampled and 400 greedy per policy, for one shared control and two trained replicas. Sampling and training repeats are averaged within each game seed before bootstrapping; shared control games are not counted as independent replications. This comparison uses fresh seeds and a pinned configuration, so earlier small-panel win rates are not its baseline.

The completed [learning study](learning_bottleneck.md) found two actionable problems: poor conditional Joker selection and a hand policy whose fixed mixture caps teacher-action probability. A fresh code review also confirms that the hand decoder reuses card logits across subset-selection steps. Its mask depends on the prefix; its learned card scores do not.

Prior work motivates two directions. [Pointer Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.03134) select input entities directly. [AlphaStar's implementation](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphastar/blob/main/alphastar/architectures/standard/heads.py) updates its unit-selection query using the selected entity embedding. These designs support testing entity-conditioned choices here; their success elsewhere does not establish a gameplay gain in Balatro. [Deep Sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.06114) motivates a shared representation for complete card subsets, which could model combinations directly.

The first implemented experiment adds an initially neutral Joker-sale residual. The entity variant scores each contextual Joker token together with the policy's global state. Its pooled control has almost the same parameter count. Both retain the original encoder and all original heads, use unchanged v13 observations, and start with zero additional logits. Architecture names are explicit in checkpoint metadata. Exact archived configurations migrate only to the known legacy head; other missing or unknown settings still fail.

All 6,415 training-sale states and 1,272 validation-sale states from the pinned current-rule dataset were encoded once. The two residual heads train on CPU with the same data order for training seeds 0 and 1. Each receives 2,000 AdamW updates, batch 320, LR 3e-4, weight decay .01, 100-step warmup and cosine decay. The objective is unit-weight conditional teacher-Joker NLL. No critic or macro-action loss is attached to this probe. Checkpoints at updates 0/100/300/1000/2000 are compared by validation NLL, with earlier steps breaking ties.

This tests whether the frozen representation contains useful information for selecting a Joker. No macro-action parameters are trained: at an identical state, sampled macro probabilities are preserved. Exact greedy mode can change the chosen action family because it ranks complete actions; sharpening Joker choices can change that ranking. Gameplay evaluates sales and all downstream consequences naturally; validation accuracy alone cannot settle the question.

Both training replicates selected the entity head at update 2,000. Both pooled controls selected update zero; continued fitting increased their validation NLL. Per-seed example ordering is identical across the entity and pooled arms. These are held-out current-rule teacher decisions, distinct from the older HF training-data audit.

The second implemented change is an energy correction over complete legal card subsets: `p_new(a|s) ∝ p_original(a|s) exp(E(s,a))`. An initially zero correction preserves the original distribution mathematically, while a learned correction can overcome the fixed mixture's probability cap and model interactions between selected cards. It uses the existing contextual card embeddings and global state, with no new observation fields or simulator scoring calls. Legal support is compacted by occupied hand-slot width, with bounded hidden-activation chunks for larger hands. The AR reference term stays in log space, retaining extremely unlikely legal actions.

The reference distribution retains both pretrained experts. Earlier pure-AR trials removed useful candidate predictions and redistributed probability between covered and uncovered labels. That motivates learning a correction around the existing policy. The experiments do not isolate the benefit of retaining that prior from the benefit of scoring complete subsets.

Likelihood, sampling, exact mode, entropy, raw-output gathering, supervised training and PPO policy reconstruction now agree on this distribution. Architecture variants `subset_residual_v1` and `entity_sale_subset_residual_v1` distinguish hand-only and combined models. Strict migrations initialize only the new parameters and preserve all original weights. Legacy mixture-ceiling diagnostics explicitly reject these variants because their old bound no longer applies. The exact categorical sampler consumes random numbers differently from the legacy sampler, even at zero correction; subset comparisons therefore include a neutral head using the same sampler.

A direct CPU re-encoding audit found 7,088 of the 11,567 validation hand decisions have valid proposals but exclude the teacher action. Their original conditional teacher probability is bounded by .5 regardless of network weights. The trained heads exceed .5001 on 3,111 / 3,137 of these decisions, with mean teacher probability on the constrained group rising from .2249 to .4404 / .4408. Seed/step/action witnesses are retained in `subset_capacity_audit.json`. This establishes that the learned predictions use probability assignments unavailable to the original fixed mixture. Isolating how much of the gameplay gain comes from that freedom requires a separate intervention. The hand experiment also changes conditional training exposure and adds trainable capacity; those effects are not individually controlled. The matched pooled-versus-entity capacity control applies to the sale experiment.

Eight retained witnesses also exceed the maximum teacher-action probability obtainable by changing only the gate between the frozen original experts. For an uncovered action, candidate probability is zero, so any such gate is bounded by the original AR probability, twice its .5-mixture probability. Both trained heads exceed that bound on these states (`subset_gate_witnesses.json`). This establishes changes beyond reweighting the frozen experts; the selected witnesses do not estimate population frequency, and do not rule out improvements from retraining the original experts with matched conditional exposure.

The next training baseline should be the combined seed-0 checkpoint, retaining v13 inputs, mixture .5 and temperature 1. Keep the encoder frozen initially and compare an actor-only continuation control with a continuation using equal numbers of current-rule teacher demonstrations and teacher corrections collected on 100 fresh student-played training seeds. Reserve new development and confirmation panels before collection, repeat training with two seeds, and judge progress by paired sampled gameplay. Corrections receive unit BC weight and no critic targets: the student's executed outcome is not a target for an unexecuted teacher action. Earlier correction trials on the weaker policy did not validate an improvement, so this is a next experiment, not an established solution.

The old critic was preserved and does not choose the actions measured here. Before considering a future PPO run, measure and, if necessary, recalibrate it separately using outcomes from actions actually executed by the stronger student. The remaining gap to teacher-level performance is unresolved; teacher panels from earlier investigations are not a matched estimate of that gap for this new checkpoint. The current evidence supports improving how available information becomes action probabilities, while leaving longer training, state-distribution differences and omitted information as open questions.
