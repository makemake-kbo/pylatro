# Controlled expansion of action-consequence labels

Hypothesis: training score/clear predictions only on the original proposal distribution leaves high-preference alternatives poorly modelled. The [training coverage audit](consequence_training_coverage_results_20260928.md) identifies 5,932 eligible selected training actions without original consequence labels, and fixes 47,125 highest-preference outside-proposal candidates. Collect these training-only labels before deciding whether the evidence warrants a fit. This is an action-coverage intervention; it adds neither games nor state contexts.

Replay the original 1,000 training games, seeds 4000000–4000999, through the frozen runtime and cached teacher decisions. Verify all 202,141 factual observations, all 47,149 teacher-play fingerprints, card-UID counters, teacher outcomes and the 1,000 recorded game terminals. For each fixed candidate, branch from a copy of the exact pre-action state and execute the candidate with the original RNG continuation. Preserve selected and retained card order. Verify parent state and card-UID isolation after every branch. The 24 states with complete existing legal coverage receive no extra branch. Retain every original label unchanged and save new labels separately.

Candidate actions were selected by the admitted fixed epoch-20 scorer from visible frozen features and action membership. The simulator supplies targets only after that selection; hidden RNG, game identity and hidden simulator state are never scorer inputs. The score target remains expected log score under the eventual supervised objective, observed through original-RNG realizations. These labels do not supply independent conditional RNG averages or an oracle policy.

A separate saved-data audit checks every selected candidate and slot list against the admitted coverage choices, every original identity and teacher outcome, all record counts, complete terminal metadata and valid outcome types. It reports descriptive errors of the frozen scorer on the additional labels and training selected-action outcomes wherever base chooses a play. The latter are completely assessable because the extra candidate equals the full-support argmax whenever that argmax is outside the original proposals. No confidence threshold or new policy is chosen from these training diagnostics.

Budget collection at 7,200 seconds plus 30 seconds supervision grace, and its audit at 900 plus 30 seconds. The original much larger collection of 184,441 new branches took 5,091 seconds; the new job adds one branch at 47,125 states while retaining full factual checks. Keep native jobs serial, admit only after actual phase and owner exits, and preserve failures. No new fit runs as part of label collection.

If labels and diagnostics justify the follow-up fit, use one exploratory model with the same 131,971-parameter pooled architecture, frozen features, seed-0 initialization, state order, 20 epochs, 7,380 updates, 942,980 state presentations, optimizer and three unit loss coefficients as the full-support control. Keep the original training-only log-score normalization. Expand each state's consequence loss from its original proposals to the original proposals plus its one new action, averaging within the state and then equally across states. This preserves total state weight while reallocating action-level supervision; it is not an intervention that leaves every original action's individual loss weight unchanged. The full-legal teacher-preference denominator and target remain unchanged. Specify and audit the augmented tensors and first-update arithmetic before fitting.

Use the fixed epoch-20 endpoint for the matched comparison, retain saved 0/5/10/20 curves, and assess consequences on both original development proposals and every newly selected development action. The same four prediction gates and four base-retention gates remain required before autonomous evaluation. Do not combine the label expansion with base anchoring, new features, new architecture, duration selection or data diversity. Use no per-change confirmation replica; final readiness still requires the independent confirmation fit and the existing fresh-win, greedy and early-attribution requirements. Better labels alone do not establish generalization.
