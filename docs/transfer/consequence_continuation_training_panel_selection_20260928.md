# Select a continuation-label decision in every training game

The [completed pilot](consequence_continuation_training_pilot_results_20260928.md) establishes reproducible labels and a measured runtime, with comparative terminal outcomes in two of its eight states. It does not estimate the prevalence of useful comparisons. Select the next collection before observing any additional terminal outcomes.

Use all 1,000 existing training seeds, 4,000,000 through 4,000,999. Select exactly one decision per game from the admitted inventory's 45,852 eligible states. For each of the eight pilot source games, retain its already selected state unconditionally and reuse its audited three continuations. For each other game, choose the eligible row minimizing SHA256 of the ASCII string `continuation-training-panel-v1:{seed}:{step}`, with step breaking a hash tie. Do not balance or resample based on future wins, immediate score, clear disagreement, pilot success, or resulting group counts. This yields 1,000 states and 3,000 action labels, of which 992 states and 2,976 branches require new continuation measurement.

Retain the inventory's exact three-action rule and selected action identities. Rejoin original and added training records to save full source snapshots and proposals for the selected states; require all identities, teacher outcomes and hashes to match. Keep all three actions even when they have equal immediate labels or when one has an already terminal label. The fitting dataset and objective are not selected by this preparation, and no development state is included.

Project new measurement time as pilot setup time, plus all 202,141 factual decisions multiplied by the pilot's factual seconds per decision, plus the number of new selected states in each group multiplied by that group's mean measured three-action rollout time. The four groups remain early/later crossed with teacher/base action agreement. Pilot timings are approximate cost estimates, not performance estimates. Project audit time using the measurement projection multiplied by the larger of one and the pilot audit/measurement runtime ratio. Freeze each phase budget at the ceiling of three times its projection, with a maximum of 28,800 seconds per phase and 30 seconds additional supervision grace. If a projected budget exceeds the cap, do not silently reduce the population or extend the cap; do not start collection under this plan.

Save the complete selection, counts, source pins, reuse pins and measured budgets with a 600-second standard-library preparation limit. No model, engine, optimizer, new continuation, or new policy runs during selection. Before the native collection starts, separately freeze its helper/auditor and require the same base controller, input restrictions, source runtime, state/RNG/UID isolation, censoring rule and full new-trace audit as the pilot. Reuse pilot evidence without repeating its scientific measurements. Existing prediction, retention and final independent-confirmation requirements remain unchanged.
