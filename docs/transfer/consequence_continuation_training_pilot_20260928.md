# Measure the fixed training-only continuation pilot

Use exactly the eight states and 24 distinct state/action entries admitted by the [training inventory](consequence_continuation_training_inventory_results_20260928.md). The pilot is already selected without new continuation outcomes. Do not replace states, add favorable cases, or make a full-collection commitment from this plan.

Reconstruct the original teacher trajectories for training seeds 4,000,000 through 4,000,055 in order, including completed games without selected states. This preserves the process-global card identity counter. Match all fourteen cached observation fields, factual actions and final metadata. At each pilot state, require the snapshot fingerprint, card UID counter, observation hash and base greedy action to reproduce their archived values.

For each of the three proposed plays, restore an independent copy of the identical original engine/history/RNG state and card counter. Take the assigned action once, then follow the immutable base checkpoint greedily with temperature 1 and candidate/AR mixture 0.5, CPU FP32, one Torch thread and batch size one. Model inputs are only the original fourteen observation keys. Seeds, engine RNG, snapshot hashes, future draws and continuation outcomes are replay/audit metadata, never predictor inputs. No new model or candidate continuation policy is introduced.

Record every action, mode, log probability, observation hash, before/after full-state fingerprint, UID counter, phase, Ante, resources and total step count. Match every field of the original immediate outcome. Historical outcomes can omit `game_won`; validate phase consistency and compare the original fields without modifying their schema. Save the first blind-completion/failure boundary and terminal win/loss/censoring. The existing 2,000-total-step limit includes the factual prefix; limit termination is censored, never a loss. First-boundary hand/discard counts reset on a clear and do not measure resources remaining in the previous blind.

Bound measurement to 900 seconds and a separate native trace/model audit to 900 seconds, each with 30 seconds supervision grace. Serialize both phases and require actual complete-owner exit before admission. The auditor independently re-forwards greedy decisions and replays every saved branch action from the original snapshots, checks all factual observations and game terminals, and verifies unchanged model tensors and absent gradients. Preserve failures; no deadline extension or automatic restart is part of this plan.

Report per-state and per-group rollout time, factual reconstruction time, branch lengths, censoring, and how many states and uncensored action pairs have different terminal wins. Also count differing terminal outcomes where the two immediate-clear labels agree. These eight selected states assess implementation, cost and whether this small sample contains comparative terminal labels. They do not estimate a policy win rate, demonstrate expected action value over hidden RNG, establish learnability, or determine a full-population prevalence. No confidence intervals, policy selection or training follow from this pilot alone. A subsequent collection must specify its sampling and measured budget separately. All prior gates, the rejected endpoint, one exploratory fit per change, and final independent confirmation requirements remain unchanged.
