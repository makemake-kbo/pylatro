# Fixed consequence prediction and retention evaluation

This is development evaluation of the [single-fit readouts](consequence_predictor_experiment_20260927.md). It is not a fresh-game or long-horizon result. Freeze workers, outputs, statistics and source/cache/checkpoint pins before measurements. Pooled is evaluated first; relational uses the identical label and feature rows.

Measure saved epochs 0, 5, 10 and 20 on all training and development proposals, with epoch 20 the fixed scientific endpoint. Export FP32 predictions in original log-score units plus clear and preference logits. Report state-balanced log-score MSE, clear BCE/Brier score and full-proposal teacher preference NLL. Report all, Ante 1–3, Ante 4+, and non-teacher/non-base alternative strata; the alternative stratum does not get an invented preference target. Padded slots never contribute. Aggregate baselines and normalization come only from training data. Recompute arithmetic and row identities independently from saved arrays.

For development contrasts against each fixed baseline, cluster complete source games. Use 10,000 shared bootstrap draws generated with `random.Random(1729).randrange(number_of_games)`, each draw sampling the original number of games with replacement. Divide summed per-state loss differences by the sampled state counts, preserving equal state weight rather than equal proposal weight. Use linear-interpolated 2.5% and 97.5% percentiles. These are descriptive development intervals without multiple-comparison adjustment. Prediction passes only for epoch 20 when both log-score MSE and clear BCE have negative point differences and strictly negative upper bounds against both baselines. Report other metrics and epoch curves without retrospectively changing the endpoint.

At epoch 20, enumerate **every legal play** for each development teacher-play state and score the preference output. Use bounded proposal chunks and reuse cached frozen features. Choose the largest preference logit, breaking exact ties by lowest action ID. When the base chooses a non-play action, preserve it and report that row explicitly. Save the complete legal action support and preference values so an independent audit can verify coverage and argmax selection. Compare overlapping proposal predictions within fixed floating-point tolerance; the head's outputs do not receive teacher identity, proposal position or outcomes as inputs.

After the prediction measurement and independent audit actually finish, reconstruct complete factual development games. Reuse admitted shared-dataset outcomes for selected actions already assessed; simulate every newly selected alternative through the full action transition. Verify factual observations and teacher outcomes, original RNG continuation, legal masks, branch inputs, parent isolation and card counters. No third action may receive an inferred or unmeasured outcome.

Retain the base's non-play actions unchanged and exclude those rows from the play-comparison denominator; they cannot produce a change from this diagnostic. For eligible play states, report base/candidate clears, early clears, total immediate losses, and newly introduced losses where the teacher survives. Retention passes only with strictly more overall clears, at least as many early clears, and no increase in either total immediate losses or newly introduced losses. Preserve gained/lost action sets and paired whole-game intervals. Immediate loss counts are distinct from eventual game wins.

The fixed endpoint must pass both prediction and retention before policy integration. A better prediction metric alone does not establish a safe policy change, resource preservation or long-horizon generalization. If both readouts pass, use the previously declared deterministic candidate rule; report relational-minus-pooled prediction and retention contrasts on identical rows before attributing a benefit to the relational readout.

Bound each arm's prediction/export/full-support pass to 1,800 seconds, independent saved-prediction audit to 1,200, retained-action replay to 1,800, and its independent audit/analysis to 1,200. Allow 1,200 seconds for the final matched comparison of both admitted readouts. Use 30 seconds of process-supervision grace per phase. The comparison uses saved endpoint predictions and complete retained-action records, checks that both arms reproduce their previously audited summaries, and reuses the tested state-balanced and whole-game arithmetic. It does not repeat neural inference or simulation. Report preference NLL differences alongside consequence errors, all/early/later/non-teacher-non-base prediction strata, and eligible all/early/later/base-match/base-disagreement retention strata. Apply the declared candidate rule without changing gates or selecting a different epoch. Run serially, preserve failures, observe actual exits, and do not retry or extend automatically. No new model fitting, policy integration, autonomous games or confirmation outcomes occur in this evaluation stage.
