# One controlled change: preference training over every legal play

The [consequence readouts](consequence_readout_results_20260928.md) improve held-out prediction on their original proposals but fail retention when choosing over every legal play. The [support diagnosis](consequence_support_results_20260928.md) localizes most lost clears and all new fatal choices to selections outside those proposals. The [saved-prefix evaluation](consequence_prefix_duration_results_20260928.md) establishes that earlier stopping is insufficient. This motivates testing the preference-training denominator before changing features or architecture again.

Train **one pooled model**, initialized identically to the original pooled fit. Change only teacher-preference cross-entropy from the original proposal set to the complete legal-play set for each training state. The teacher remains the target; additional legal actions are competitors, not invented score/clear labels. Retain the same state-balanced score MSE and clear BCE on the original recorded-continuation proposals, normalization, unit loss weights, frozen features and 131,971-parameter head. No hidden RNG enters the model. Keep the same 47,149 states, original epoch orders, 20 epochs, 128-state optimizer batches, 7,380 updates, 942,980 state presentations, seed zero, AdamW learning rate .001 / betas .9,.999 / epsilon 1e-8 / weight decay zero, global gradient clipping at one, CPU FP32 and one Torch thread. Save epochs 0, 5, 10 and 20; the primary comparison is the fixed epoch-20 endpoint against the admitted original pooled endpoint. Duration, data diversity, base-preservation penalties and independently trainable features are separate interventions.

Use source-pinned original action masks to enumerate each state's exact legal plays. Bind membership, state/cache identity, ragged offsets/action IDs and proposal positions to the admitted feature and label artifacts. Every original labeled action must appear in its state's legal support. Preserve legal action ordering and the teacher's actual position within it.

The technical preflight performs no optimizer steps. Test state-balanced losses and output gradients against an independent analytic reference, full parameter gradients against per-state dense evaluation, exclusion of illegal/padded actions, supervision masks and complete-state microbatch packing. Then verify every training state's legal support and map, and check a real initial batch against float64 saved-output arithmetic. Compare shared proposal predictions and score/BCE losses with the original proposal-only forward. Benchmark four predeclared original optimizer batches (first three and the batch containing the largest legal support in epoch one); estimate complete-fit cost from all original batches' padded cells. These checks concern implementation and feasibility, not learning success.

Memory is bounded by grouping complete states into at most 8,192 padded action cells per forward. All microbatch contributions divide by the original optimizer batch's number of states. Accumulate gradients, then clip and step exactly once per original batch. No state's preference denominator may be truncated or sampled. Microbatch numerical roundoff is accepted within the declared reference tolerances; the mathematical objective and update cadence stay fixed.

Freeze the fit helper, independent saved-fit auditor, cost-based time bound and evaluation protocol before training. Preserve failed artifacts and require actual worker and owner exits before admission. One exploratory fit is allowed; independent confirmation waits for a final candidate. The saved-fit audit must verify initial weights, full action support/proposal mapping, complete state order/exposure, output loss gradients, first-update clipping/Adam arithmetic and checkpoint optimizer steps. It must not train a second model.

Evaluation retains the established four prediction gates and four action-retention gates on the already exposed development games: paired improvements in score MSE and clear BCE over both training-only baselines; more overall clears than base, at least as many early clears, no increase in total immediate losses or newly introduced losses where the teacher survives. Score all legal plays, retain non-play base actions, and assess every chosen play through an admitted identical outcome or full simulator branch. Also report matched differences against the original pooled epoch-20 scorer. Only passing prediction AND retention permits later policy integration and fresh games. The original fresh sampled-win, greedy retention, early-attribution and final independent confirmation requirements remain unchanged.

This addresses a mismatch introduced in the new scorer. The existing subset policy already normalizes over full legal support, so a successful repair would not by itself explain the original pretraining gap or establish long-horizon generalization.
