# Full-support preference training passes its implementation checks

The [controlled full-support experiment](consequence_full_support_20260928.md) is technically ready. Its preflight passed four tests covering analytic losses/output derivatives, full parameter gradients against per-state dense evaluation, invalid proposal positions, and memory-bounded grouping of complete states. The preflight performed **zero optimizer updates** and left the original initial model unchanged.

Every one of the **47,149 training states** was matched to the original recorded observation row. The support contains **12,249,244 legal plays**, ranging from one to 3,472 per state. All **245,637 original labeled proposals** map to their exact legal action and selected-card membership. Saved ragged action arrays preserve ordering and the teacher's position; they neither sample competitors nor introduce consequence labels for previously unlabeled actions.

On the first original 128-state optimizer batch, independent float64 arithmetic reproduced the three losses and every output gradient. The maximum absolute gradient discrepancy was **4.66 × 10⁻¹⁰**. Score loss was 1.141482 and clear BCE 0.678448, matching the original proposal-only forward within the declared tolerance. Preference CE changes from 1.664645 to 5.481750 because its denominator now includes 35,365 legal plays across those states. That numerical increase is expected from the larger denominator; it is not a performance comparison.

Four predeclared original batches took 0.137–0.190 seconds each for the loss/backward computation. The complete original 20-epoch schedule contains 244,984,880 legal-action presentations, 443,478,075 padded action cells and 65,351 microbatches, accumulated into the same **7,380 optimizer updates and 942,980 state presentations**. The projected backward cost is about 1,121 seconds, excluding input copying, optimizer steps, checkpoints and system variation. The fit is bounded to 3,600 seconds, followed by a separately bounded independent saved-fit audit.

All preflight worker and owner exits were observed as zero before admission. The [preflight result](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/full_support_preflight_v1/result_v1.json) contains counts, arithmetic checks and timings; its [owner receipt](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/full_support_preflight_owner_terminal_v1.json) records admission. Runtime was 8.880 seconds.

The **single exploratory fit completed its updates but failed the final runtime-origin check** under the [frozen fit protocol](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/full_support_fit_protocol_v1.json). It keeps the original pooled architecture, initial weights, frozen features, recorded-continuation labels, normalization, state orders, optimizer settings and exposure. The primary endpoint remains epoch 20; epochs 0/5/10 are saved for the fitting curve. The sole scientific change is full-legal-support teacher-preference CE. Evaluation rules were fixed before training. Draft evaluation helpers retain the original prediction/retention gates and add paired comparisons with the original pooled endpoint; prior verified state/action outcomes can be reused by exact identity.

Implementation correctness and feasible runtime do not establish transfer. The [separate saved-artifact recovery](consequence_full_support_recovery_20260928.md) passed all fit audits with zero additional optimizer steps; the original failed exit remains preserved. The [completed held-out evaluation](consequence_full_support_results_20260928.md) passes prediction but fails retention. Fresh-game attribution and final independent confirmation have not been reached. No long-horizon or win-rate improvement is claimed.
