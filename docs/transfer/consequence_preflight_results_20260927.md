# Consequence-scoring preparation passes; scientific readout study completed

The subsequent [controlled readout study](consequence_readout_results_20260928.md) is now complete: both prediction screens pass, but both action-retention screens fail. The evidence below records the successful technical preparation; it does not establish a policy or win-rate gain.

The [duration experiment](continuation_duration_results_20260927.md) failed its autonomous advancement screen. Following the agreed sequence, the consequence-label audit, hidden-RNG input check and frozen-feature benchmark are now complete. These are technical results, not improvements in learned predictions or win rate.

The label audit reconstructs all 3,222 decisions and 769 teacher plays in the first 16 current-rule training games. It assesses 4,020 distinct state–action proposals through 8,040 plain/instrumented branches. Every pair matches on visible inputs, score, clear/loss status, full state fingerprint and card counters; factual observations and historical teacher/base outcomes also match. Instrumentation observes 1,862 internal scoring-state copies used by the Joker-ordering harness and exactly one committed score per action. The independent saved-row verifier passes, and both workers and the owner actually exit zero. See the [label verification](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/label_verification_v1.json) and [owner receipt](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/label_owner_terminal_v1.json).

Random APIs occur before or during scoring on 1,077 proposals. The separate input check replaces the hidden RNG with four precisely defined fresh continuations per proposal, preserving the harness's probe/commit coupling. All **16,080 intervention branches retain identical model-visible inputs**. Against the recorded continuation, score changes on 2,903 branch realizations involving 908 distinct state–action proposals; clear status changes on 207 realizations involving 109 proposals; immediate loss changes on six realizations involving three proposals. The [independent recount](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/rng_input_verification_v1.json) and [actual exits](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/rng_input_owner_terminal_v1.json) pass. This is a training-pilot intervention, not a conditional RNG-law estimate or a proof that unchanged examples are deterministic.

The first predictor will therefore treat recorded log-scores and clear indicators as noisy samples, targeting conditional expected log-score and immediate-clear probability under its training distribution. The four stress draws are excluded from scientific training. Full details and limitations are in the pinned [target definition](consequence_stochastic_targets_20260927.md).

The pooled prototype has **131,971 trainable parameters** over a frozen 384-dimensional, 12-layer base transformer. On 32 training states / 158 proposals, the extracted state, hand cards and contextual tokens exactly match the original agent forward path. A single CPU-thread feature batch takes 0.882 seconds; head forward/backward takes 0.019 seconds. These are one-batch timings, not a full-run throughput claim. Gradients are finite, the base has no gradients, base/head tensors remain unchanged, and no optimizer runs. Grouped versus individual predictions differ by at most 3.13e-7; padded hand slots have no effect. See the [benchmark result](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/feature_benchmark_v1/result_v1.json) and [actual owner exit](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/feature_benchmark_owner_terminal_v1.json).

The matched relational prototype has **132,035 parameters**, only 64 more. Four native technical checks verify capacity, masked-context independence and zero masked gradients, finite gradients through attention, proposal grouping, joint reindexing of contextual features/masks, and rejection of empty context. Its [test receipt](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/relational_tests_terminal_v1.json) records the actual zero exit. Those tests use synthetic features, perform no optimizer step, and do not compare learned performance.

The [shared label pipeline](consequence_dataset_20260927.md) is complete and independently verified. Both measurement workers and both saved-row auditors exited zero, followed by the actual owner exit. Every factual observation and teacher outcome reproduced, with branch inputs, parent state and card counters checked. The [completion record](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/dataset_complete_v1.json) and [owner receipt](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/dataset_owner_terminal_v1.json) bind the results.

Training measurement took 5,090.9 seconds and its audit 55.0 seconds; development measurement took 595.3 seconds and its audit 7.1 seconds. These verify the training/evaluation labels; they do not show learned prediction or policy gains.

The [frozen scientific recipe](consequence_predictor_experiment_20260927.md) keeps the original 1,000 current-rule training games fixed and requires both prediction and retention gains before policy integration. Shared feature extraction completed both splits and independent audits, with 3,233,322,972 array bytes, unchanged base tensors and no base gradients. Training extraction/audit took 1131.4/5.9 seconds; development took 143.1/0.9 seconds. See the [feature completion](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/features_complete_v1.json) and [actual owner exit](../../eval/consequence_prediction_20260927/features_owner_terminal_v1.json).

Four stdlib checks verify state-balanced training statistics and shared epoch ordering. Four further metric checks cover padding, extreme logits, unequal proposal counts, baseline fallback and whole-game weighting. The [evaluation workers](consequence_evaluation_20260927.md) have now completed saved-prefix predictions, every legal endpoint play, all newly chosen action outcomes and the matched readout comparison. Each readout received one fit. Their [scientific results](consequence_readout_results_20260928.md) show improved consequence prediction but failed retention; no policy integration, autonomous games or confirmation fit followed. The long-horizon objective remains unproven.
