# Stochastic targets and a hidden-RNG input check

The first consequence predictor will use full-action replay outcomes under the recorded simulator continuation. For a visible observation and proposed play, squared error on `log1p(score)` estimates the conditional mean of that transformed score under the training distribution. Binary cross-entropy on the immediate blind-clear indicator estimates its conditional probability. A single outcome is a noisy observation; neither label is an exact expected-action ordering. The log target is not the logarithm of expected score, and immediate clear is not eventual victory. Teacher preference supervision remains a separate objective because resource preservation and growth may favor a lower immediate score.

No game seed, hidden random state, branch outcome, post-action feature, label-derived feature, or random-call trace may enter model features. Both pooled and relational readouts must use the same frozen encoder and the same allowed preaction observation fields. Split entire source games, including every alternative and random realization, together. The base encoder has previously seen the later training corpus; a split of that corpus alone cannot be described as unseen-game transfer evidence. The already exposed development corpus also cannot provide final confirmation.

Before fitting, run a bounded technical input-invariance check on the same first 16 training games and all proposals from the completed native label audit. For each teacher-play state, create four additional random continuations. Each continuation is a fresh native `PseudorandomState` initialized from the SHA-256 hexadecimal digest of the fixed string `consequence-rng-stress-v1|<visible observation hash>|<draw index>`, with draw indices 0–3. Its channel map starts empty; the simulator advances it normally. Use the same initial continuation for every proposal at that state/draw, and preserve native deep-copy coupling between Joker-ordering probes and the committed action. Do not resample individual probes or scorer invocations.

This explicit distribution is an intervention used to test input separation and expose target variation. It is **not** a posterior over hidden continuations conditional on the visible state or a claim that fresh channel states reproduce the original game's conditional law. These four intervention outcomes will not become extra scientific training labels or be used to estimate calibrated environment probabilities. A later decision to use resampled training labels would require a separately declared target distribution.

Require exact equality of all policy-visible input bytes after replacing RNG, unchanged card counters from observation construction, intact factual and snapshot states, and exact reproduction of factual observations and teacher outcomes. Use complete action transitions and record only score/clear/loss outcomes with source identity, action, draw index and hashes for audit. Compare intervention outcomes with the audited original realization and recount changed scores, clear labels and immediate losses. No observed difference is evidence of equality only for those tested draws, not proof of determinism or observation sufficiency.

Run the worker serially after the label audit's actual successful owner exit and independent saved-row verification. Cap measurement at 900 seconds and saved-row verification at 300 seconds, with 30 seconds of process-supervision grace per phase. Retain failed artifacts and stop on mismatch. No model training, autonomous evaluation, candidate selection or confirmation evidence. This audit leaves the feature benchmark and fixed scientific fitting recipe to complete next.
