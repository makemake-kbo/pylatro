# Frozen scorer alignment with terminal continuation labels

The wider scorer learns immediate consequences better, while preference transfer and retention remain weak. The existing training continuation panel contains 416 winning/losing action pairs; 291 share their immediate-clear label, and winning actions sometimes score less immediately. The unresolved mechanism is target mismatch: do the current outputs rank these delayed distinctions usefully? The competing explanation is that they already capture the distinctions and policy integration or autonomous state coverage dominates.

Before another fit, evaluate the frozen epoch-20 wider scorer on all 1,000 previously selected training states and their three audited branches. Extract its 384-dimensional action-conditioned activation after `readout[1]` for possible later use, together with immediate score, clear logit, centered preference and base log probability. Use only the five approved cached observation features as model inputs. Seeds, identities and terminal outcomes remain metadata/targets. Preserve card and Joker order and check selected/retained slots.

Use the existing canonical CPU FP32 one-state full-support inference, ascending legal actions in chunks of 512, unchanged checkpoint and initial reference. Require exact agreement with every overlapping original-proposal output. An independent auditor recomputes all extracted features and outputs in the same layout, verifies source identities and terminal comparisons, and recounts summaries. No optimization, new simulation, policy integration, checkpoint selection, numerical tolerance changes or advancement-gate changes.

Report correct/tied/reversed ordering for score, clear, preference and base on all 416 known win/loss pairs and the fixed same-clear, early/later and base/teacher-agreement partitions. Count exact ties separately; report pair and state-weighted ordering. Observed immediate score/clear are diagnostic comparators, not deployable policies. Report restricted three-action choices with deterministic smallest-global-action tie breaking, explicitly noting teacher-containing proposals. For full-support preference choices, report coverage and unknown-outcome bounds where the selected action lacks a continuation label. Do not substitute a labelled action for an unlabelled selected action.

Budget: one serialized inference/audit owner, at most 600 seconds per phase plus 30 seconds supervisor grace; expected cost is tens of seconds given the completed 430-state extraction. No broad polling or parallel native job. Admit only after the complete owner exits successfully.

Decision: this diagnostic can establish an output/target mismatch and prepare frozen features. It cannot establish learnability or generalization. A future terminal readout requires a separately frozen fit protocol, action-sensitive held-out evaluation on source games unseen by the feature fit, and retention evidence before integration. A split of these 1,000 games alone is insufficient because all contributed to the frozen feature pathway. Existing rejected candidates stay rejected. The fresh sampled/greedy win-rate, early-attribution and final independent-confirmation requirements remain unchanged.
