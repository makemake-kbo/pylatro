# Separate state-outcome error from action-outcome error

The RMS readout fits training labels but remains worse than the ante baseline on terminal-label validation. Before another fit, use its admitted outputs to test whether the excess error mainly comes from shared state confidence, poorly estimated differences between actions, or both. The competing explanations are a state-calibration problem and insufficient transferable action information. These accounting measurements cannot by themselves identify irreducible RNG noise or prove a new architecture will help.

For every saved state, decompose Brier error into error of the three-action mean probability and error of centered action probabilities. Relative to the fixed ante baseline, the second component is exactly `mean(delta_p²) - 2*mean(delta_p*delta_y)`. Decompose BCE into the common-mean-logit error, the nonnegative softplus dispersion cost, and the target-aligned action term. Reconcile both decompositions to the already admitted losses without changing any prediction.

For mixed-outcome states, report conditional terminal-label NLL given the number of winning branches. With three proposals this is softmax over the winning action when one branch wins, or softmax over negative logits for the losing action when two win. Shared state-logit shifts cancel. Compare to uniform log(3), the base scores and the existing preference scores without fitting a temperature or any coefficient. Report all/early/later groups and game-bootstrap intervals for validation. This is a diagnostic target, not a new advancement gate.

Measure between-state and within-state feature/label/logit variance. Decompose the training BCE gradient at initialization and the fixed endpoint into state-mean and centered-action terms; verify their sum and the endpoint regularization balance. Gradient norms/cosines describe the current objective and do not establish harmful optimization conflict. Preserve all branches; uniform-outcome states still contain training information even though they have no observed win/loss contrast.

Inspect coarse action-comparison coverage using outcome-free bins: ante bucket (1–3, 4–6, 7+), last-hand indicator, sorted selected-card counts and selected-slot overlap count. For every pair in the fixed training/validation split, count training source games and mixed terminal comparisons in its bin, excluding the same game for training queries. Report validation terminal-contrast support counts and unsupported comparisons, including early/later. These coarse bins omit ranks, Joker interactions and other context; matching a bin is not proof of sufficient mechanical coverage.

One bounded saved-array analysis, maximum 120 seconds, no optimization, simulator, encoder inference, policy, clipping, row removal or gate change. Verify exact identities and all previously admitted aggregate losses before accepting new decompositions. This uses the exposed 800/200 terminal-label split; all its games contributed to the frozen feature fit. Original-RNG outcomes remain controller-dependent realized labels. Any result leaves current rejected candidates rejected and preserves the full unseen-game/retention/fresh-win/early-attribution/confirmation requirements.
