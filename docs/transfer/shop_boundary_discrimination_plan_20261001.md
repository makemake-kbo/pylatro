# Distinguishing weak confidence from weak opportunity discrimination

The completed boundary-supervision experiment detects no beneficial opportunities at its fixed indicator threshold, but its average forecast on positive pairs exceeds its average on negative pairs. Before attributing this to a representation failure, measure separation without choosing a new operating threshold.

Use only the already admitted training and development forecasts, raw native gains, masks and metadata. Do not fit a model, calibrator or threshold, clip outputs, make model/simulator calls or change the rejected candidate's disposition. A state is positive when at least one **observed** gain exceeds 0.03. Compare maximum indicator forecast with maximum raw regression forecast on exactly this observed support. This oracle support is a diagnostic and is unavailable at policy inference. A maximum marginal pair forecast is a ranking score, not a calibrated probability that a state has a beneficial replacement.

Report state ROC AUC and average precision, using half credit for AUC ties and threshold-grouped precision for AP ties. Report state prevalence, class-conditional score quantiles at 0, .1, .25, .5, .75, .9 and 1, and paired differences against the prior dense regression. Use all 128 complete development games for 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples, seed 1729; preserve equal decision weights and explicitly count draws lacking either class. Training results are descriptive, without intervals. No alternative pooling functions, selected subsets or selected thresholds.

For the indicator's original pair forecasts, report fixed bins (-infinity,0], (0,.1], ..., (.9,1], (1,infinity). Each labelled decision contributes total weight one across its observed pairs. Report bin support, mean original forecast and observed positive frequency; these bins were fixed before reading these outcomes. Also split the existing Brier-form error into positive-pair and nonpositive-pair contributions on the original equal-decision denominator. Reconstruct the admitted Brier result exactly. Do not infer an irreducible error floor from either calibration or these heuristic labels.

Independently verify ranking arithmetic, ties, bootstrap intervals, calibration bins and accounting.
