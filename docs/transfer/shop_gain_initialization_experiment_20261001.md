# Initialize the relative-gain readout from its training-only solution

The [frozen-feature probe](shop_gain_readout_probe_results_20261001.md) establishes unused predictive information in the existing feature basis. Solving the same 64 gain-output weights reduces training centered error by 8.4%, and development raw centered error from 0.0440102 to 0.0399464. Conditional owned-slot agreement on observable support improves from 29.73% to 37.13%, a paired gain of 7.40 percentage points [4.12, 10.62]. On teacher-sale states, same-offer removal concordance rises from 58.84% to 64.12% [difference 3.46, 7.14 points]. This establishes a partial auxiliary-readout optimization gap; the gameplay policy was unchanged and remains rejected.

Test one initialization intervention in the otherwise unchanged relative-consequence training recipe. Reconstruct the original base plus seed-zero relation and zero auxiliary head. Before joint training, extract its initial 64-dimensional pair features on the original training corpus and solve only the gain-output row against the original relative objective. Install that solution, cast once to native float32, then execute exactly the original 1,000 joint updates. Do not initialize from the rejected endpoint or reuse its solved coefficients: its feature basis has changed during training.

Keep architecture, parameter names/count/order, original base, 1,000 games, dense target pack, masks, normalizers, four-task weighting, full-action and future-purchase losses, original row stream, batch320/micro32, seed zero, MPS float32 and evaluation mode with gradients. Keep the actor, embeddings, backbone and auxiliary/relation parameters trainable and the critic fixed. Use the same AdamW3e-5, betas(.9,.999), epsilon1e-8, zero weight decay, 100-update warmup and 1,000-update endpoint. The completed zero-initialized relative run is the matched control and is not refitted.

The intervention includes an extra training-label pass used only to initialize 64 coefficients. It preserves joint-update count and joint row exposure, not total supervision exposure. Report that distinction explicitly. This is one exploratory model-training pipeline, with one deterministic head-initialization solve, not an additional policy candidate or independent confirmation. No learning-rate, duration, coefficient, cutoff, width or eligibility sweep; no periodic refitting during the joint run.

Use the already tested CPU float64 minimum-norm least-squares calculation with fixed rcond1e-12. For each state, center its features and stored standardized float32 gain targets on the observed final-comparison pairs. Divide the design and response by sqrt(n*202141). Empty and singleton sets contribute zero. Keep gain bias and the other ten output rows at zero during initialization; only row two of the existing 11-by-64 auxiliary weight tensor changes. Preserve meaningful entity order. Model construction, feature collection and installation must not consume the subsequent training RNG stream. No hidden card IDs, seeds, RNG state, future outcomes or teacher support masks enter the forward path.

Initialization uses training data only. Freeze extraction, native preflight, installation, joint-fit and evaluation protocols before their respective execution. Verify the untouched extended initial state matches the original relative experiment before installation; every other tensor remains byte-identical afterward. Check the initial action and prospective-purchase losses against the original matched run. Independently verify the first gain-weight gradient and the now-nonzero gain contribution to feature gradients; do not retain the zero-head auditor’s assumption that auxiliary feature gradients vanish. Verify native float32 installation, strict checkpoint provenance, optimizer state, all updates, row exposure and critic retention. Numerical or technical failures must be preserved; corrections get new source versions rather than a scientific retry chosen from outcomes.

The initial training-feature extraction must remain read-only, use original consecutive batches of 32 and complete before the head solve. Preserve strict source/input/checkpoint pins, lifecycle bounds, durable logs and actual-exit evidence. Determine and freeze operational time bounds from the completed feature-extraction and fit receipts before launch. Do not open the reserved confirmation outcomes.

Evaluate the final joint endpoint on the original complete training/development panels with the same thirteen policy checks, original controls, matched zero-initialized relative endpoint and its own correction-disabled ablation. Report centered gain prediction, observed-pair ranking/regret, observable versus oracle-supported owned-slot agreement, and the three fixed comparison axes for all gain-labelled and teacher-sale states. Use all 128 complete development games in 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples with seed1729. Gain offsets remain unconstrained; do not introduce an absolute-gain threshold or directly replace policy logits with consequence predictions.

Any failed original policy check rejects the endpoint before fresh gameplay or an independent confirmation fit. Prediction gains alone are insufficient. Full success still requires fresh sampled win improvement of at least five percentage points against both base and original ordinary4k with paired lower bounds above zero; greedy lower bounds strictly above minus five points against both; positive learned early-policy attribution and independent-fit direction; and one final independent fit plus untouched confirmation. No world-model work is included.

This tests whether better initial auxiliary optimization produces a better joint learning trajectory. Success would not establish that initialization is the only bottleneck; failure would not erase the demonstrated frozen-feature prediction gain. Capacity, retained-build interactions, noisy heuristic targets and objective weighting remain possible contributors, without an architecture intervention being combined into this experiment.
