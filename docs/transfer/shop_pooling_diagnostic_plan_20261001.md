# Can offer pooling explain the remaining sale errors?

The completed event-supervision experiment improves opportunity discrimination on the exposed development panel (AUC .924390, AP .658365), but fails six original policy checks. Its forecasts are training-only outputs. This diagnostic uses the already audited saved policy traces to distinguish weak pair corrections from attenuation by their offer-weighted average. It trains nothing, calls no model or simulator, and cannot qualify this rejected endpoint for gameplay.

Freeze the following calculations for the event endpoint, with both the original training and development populations. Use every active replacement state and preserve all complete games in bootstrap denominators. Do not select thresholds, fit a calibrator, or swap pooling at policy inference.

1. Compare the existing policy's SALE-family log odds with the auxiliary maximum-event forecast as scores for teacher SALE. Report ROC AUC and average precision, with paired complete-game intervals on development. These scores measure different readouts of shared features; a ranking gap does not establish the effect of connecting them.
2. On active teacher SALE states, record full-policy agreement and conditional owned-slot agreement before and after the correction. Conditional agreement uses uniform ties among maximizing owned slots. This does not imply that the model chooses to sell.
3. For the teacher-owned slot, calculate its current log odds against legal LEAVE. The joint-action tilt gives these odds exactly as `macro_sale + sale_teacher - logsumexp(legal_sale_logits) - macro_leave + residual_teacher`. Check the formula independently through normalized corrected macro and conditional distributions, against the archived teacher NLL, and reconstruct the archived residual and macro delta.
4. Replace the teacher's residual only in the mathematical bound with the maximum of its existing pair scores over visible offers. Every convex offer average is at most this maximum. If even this upper bound loses to LEAVE, no reweighting of the existing pairs can make the teacher action the mode. Count that obstruction among all active teacher sales and among their current errors. Conversely, an upper bound above LEAVE is only necessary, not sufficient, for correct full action selection.
5. Independently bound competition with other legal owned slots: maximize the teacher's correction and minimize each competitor's correction. If the teacher still loses, even independently chosen convex weights for each owned slot cannot make it win. The actual shared proposal has tighter constraints. Record the union of the LEAVE and owned-slot impossibility conditions, and the subset where the current teacher loses to LEAVE but its maximum-pair bound clears LEAVE.
6. Report teacher pair-score spread, maximum-minus-current correction, and the correction required to beat LEAVE. These put any averaging attenuation in the scale of the existing decision margin.

Use fixed numerical tolerance 2e-6 for strict impossibility/crossing statements, 2e-5 for archived native NLL agreement. Ties/near ties are not certified impossible. A single legal owned slot has no owned-slot obstruction. Exclude a LEAVE comparison only if the audited macro mask makes it illegal, and report its denominator. No missing or hidden information enters a learner.

Use the existing tested aggregation and ranking routines: 10,000 resamples of all 128 development games, seed 1729; training point estimates over all 1,000 games. Marginal intervals are descriptive, without multiplicity correction. Verify the algebra on synthetic cases with masked offers, tied scores, one owned slot and conflicting offer preferences, plus independently normalized probabilities on every actual teacher-sale row. Pin inputs and source before running.

These are bounds on this fixed learned endpoint. They cannot establish that pooling is harmless after retraining or that architecture is sufficient. Normalized log-sum-exp pooling is also bounded by the maximum pair score; this diagnosis does not evaluate or authorize an unnormalized sum, added bias, threshold sweep, new fit or fresh gameplay. All original policy and final win/early-attribution/confirmation requirements remain.
