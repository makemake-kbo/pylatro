# Bounded teacher-utility diagnosis

This diagnostic asks whether the teacher's missed SHOP sales have a clear advantage under its own existing heuristic comparisons. It does not measure eventual win value or qualify the rejected sale-family model for gameplay.

Use the first four games by seed in each existing original-training and exposed-development cache. Within those eight games, include every active teacher-sale state and the first two active teacher-non-sale states per game. Selection uses the already frozen sale-family eligibility indices; no margin or prediction determines inclusion. Replay every recorded action through each selected game and require exact equality of all fourteen observation fields at every step. No autonomous policy acts.

For each target, query a fresh original teacher and a fresh observed teacher on separate copies, restoring the card UID allocator after each query. Require their choices to equal each other and the saved teacher action. Require the factual runner, observation, UID allocator, Python/NumPy RNG and Torch CPU RNG (if loaded) to remain unchanged by queries. Failure is recorded and stops the diagnostic; do not quietly omit mismatches.

The observer inserts a callback before exactly three native gain comparisons in ShopSearch.select: standalone sales, planet purchases and Joker purchase/replacement plans. Removing those callbacks must reproduce the original AST exactly. Callbacks copy scalar values only. Preserve native feasibility checks, candidate iteration order and strict greater-than tie handling. Reconstruct the sequence of incumbent best values independently from recorded candidates. No production or archived source is edited.

Record every candidate reaching those comparisons, its action, owned/offer identities, heuristic gain, feasibility and incumbent. Safety-rejected replacement plans that never reach a gain comparison remain unscored. Aggregate replacement plans by immediate action using their maximum feasible gain. Report the winning gain above the teacher's existing 0.03 cutoff, the gap to the best other scored action, and the gap to the saved model choice only where that action has a comparable recorded score. A fallback purchase or end-shop action does not acquire an invented utility of zero. Report unscored coverage explicitly.

Use the admitted sale-family endpoint's saved actions for diagnosis only. Report training and development separately, including exact-model-match, wrong-Joker-rank and correct-Joker-rank/full-error groups. Show raw counts and continuous margin summaries; choose no close-call cutoff, new gate, alternate decoder, coefficient, fit or policy from these values. Small fixed panels are illustrative, not population estimates. No hidden RNG, game seed or future outcome enters a model.

One serialized native worker, maximum 1,800 active seconds plus 30 owner seconds, with the existing explicit pause/resume controller. Sources, plan and selected indices are pinned before execution. Admit output only after the whole owner exits successfully and a separate saved-record check verifies counts, candidate reductions and source/input hashes. Keep any failed source, protocol and partial outputs. No second exploratory training run, fresh-game evaluation or confirmation is part of this diagnosis.
